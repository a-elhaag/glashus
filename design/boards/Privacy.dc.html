Glashus exists to show you who can see your files. This page says what we can see, and why.

1. What we collect

  • Account: name and email from Firebase Authentication.
  • Files: what you upload, plus name, size, type and versions.
  • Scan findings: the type and location of sensitive data. Values are stored masked.
  • Activity: uploads, shares, deletions and sign-ins, kept in your audit log.
  • Link visits: time, IP address and browser of people who open your shared links.

2. How we use it

To store and show your files, scan them, compute your exposure score, and alert you to risky changes. We do not sell your data or show ads.

3. AI processing

AI features are off until you allow them, per folder. When allowed, the needed text or image is sent to Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI with detected sensitive values masked first. Every read is written to your AI access log.
